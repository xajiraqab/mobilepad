Touchpad gestures

Simple controls, just like a laptop touchpad.

Tap

Tap once for left click.

Long press

Press and hold for right click.

Two-finger scroll

Use two fingers to scroll up and down.

Drag & select

Double-tap, hold, and drag to select text or move items.
